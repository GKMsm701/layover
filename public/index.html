THE MOST IMPORTANT QUEST

Mystery is fun.
Safety isn't a mystery.

SoulQuest should make exploration exciting without compromising anyone's privacy or comfort.

⌖

Public places only

Choose familiar, accessible places with other people around. Never follow a clue into an isolated area or private property.

◉

No live tracking

Other travellers' exact live locations stay private. Location access should be optional, transparent and limited to what's needed.

♡

Consent at every step

A match is not permission to meet. Both people must accept a meetup invitation, and either can change their mind.

⚑

Leave or report

You can leave any quest. In this demo, report and block controls show the intended flow but don't contact a moderation team.

◇
Demo safety noticeFictional users and sample locations only. Do not use this prototype to arrange a real-world meeting.
